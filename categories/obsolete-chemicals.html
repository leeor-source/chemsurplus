obsolete chemicals disposal — FAQ

Are reused chemicals still considered waste?

Generally no — if someone can legitimately reclaim or use them, the chemicals are not a waste under hazardous-waste rules. Documentation (SDS/COA) supports the transfer.

What if my chemicals can't be reused?

If no reuse is possible they must go to a permitted hazardous-waste facility. ChemSurplus helps you exhaust the reuse option first.
