Why surplus instead of disposal?

When chemicals become excess, obsolete or off-spec, disposal is expensive and wasteful. Regulators including the US EPA recommend finding a legitimate reuse before disposal — chemicals that are reclaimed or reused are generally not classified as hazardous waste. A surplus marketplace is the most efficient way to find that reuse: sellers recover value and avoid disposal fees, buyers cut input costs, and usable chemistry stays in the economy instead of being incinerated.
