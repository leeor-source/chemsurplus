The economy

The flywheel

Onboarding a broker's catalog is cheap supply. A richer, normalized, industry-tagged catalog pulls more buyers; more buyers mean faster deals, which pulls in more brokers. We bootstrap supply by aggregating existing brokers, then spend the real effort on demand — supply-aggregated, demand-led.

Liquidity milestone: ~500 closed transactions per industry is the flip-point to becoming the default channel. We hit it lane by lane.
