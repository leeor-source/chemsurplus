The 2025 wildcard

US ethane export curbs to China (China was ~47% of US ethane exports in 2024) are stranding feedstock onshore and idling some Chinese ethane-only crackers — increasing US domestic surplus and volatility. That means more distressed, must-move material originating on the US Gulf Coast: good supply for a surplus platform, and a widening gap for European and Asian buyers to capture.

Sources: S&P Global Chemical Trends, McKinsey, Cefic, US EIA, ICIS, ChemAnalyst, Intratec. Figures indicative; refresh quarterly.
