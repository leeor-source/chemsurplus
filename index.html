FAQ

Surplus chemicals, answered

What is a surplus chemicals marketplace?

A surplus chemicals marketplace connects companies that have excess, obsolete, off-spec or short-dated chemical inventory with buyers who can use it. Sellers recover value and avoid disposal costs; buyers source quality chemicals well below market price.

How much can I save buying surplus chemicals?

Surplus and spot chemical lots typically sell 30–70% below current wholesale pricing, depending on quantity, shelf life and packaging.

Do listings include SDS and COA documentation?

Yes. Every ChemSurplus lot is published with a Safety Data Sheet (SDS), and most include a Certificate of Analysis (COA) or Technical Data Sheet for full traceability.

What kinds of chemicals can I sell?

Solvents, specialty chemicals, resins & coatings, surfactants, acids & bases, polymers & additives, lab reagents and intermediates — in drums, totes, supersacks, pails or bulk.
