# SOUL.md — ChemSurplus

> The identity of the product. Anything user-facing — copy, design, a new page, an email — should be able to trace back to this. If a change contradicts the soul, the soul wins.

## Mission
Keep usable chemistry in the economy. Turn the chemicals industry's surplus, excess and obsolete inventory from a disposal liability into recovered value and affordable supply.

## Why we exist
Every year, perfectly usable chemicals are incinerated or landfilled because the company holding them can't find the next user fast enough. Meanwhile, smaller formulators and labs overpay for prime material they don't strictly need. ChemSurplus closes that gap. Regulators (incl. the US EPA) already say *find a reuse before disposal* — we make that the easy, default path.

## The two people we serve
**Dana — the seller (first user).** Plant / inventory / EHS manager at a manufacturer, distributor or lab. Has drums, totes and supersacks of overstock, discontinued, off-spec or short-dated stock. Measured on freed warehouse space, avoided disposal spend, and recovered write-downs. Wants: list it fast, stay compliant, get a real offer.

**Sam — the buyer (secondary).** Procurement lead or formulator at a smaller manufacturer or contract lab. Measured on input cost. Wants: quality chemistry, real documentation, a price well below market, no surprises.

One marketplace, two jobs: **value out** for Dana, **supply in** for Sam.

## Values (in priority order)
1. **Trust before volume.** SDS/COA on every lot, verified counterparties. We'd rather list fewer, documented lots than many dubious ones.
2. **Reuse over disposal.** The circular-economy outcome is the point, not a tagline.
3. **Radical clarity.** Plain numbers — quantity, grade, shelf life, price, packaging, region — no buried terms.
4. **Speed for the seller.** Dead stock loses value every day. Listing takes minutes; verification is fast.
5. **Compliance is non-negotiable.** Hazmat handling, accurate description, legal transfer — always.

## Voice & tone
- **Confident, concrete, commercial.** We speak to professionals who buy chemicals for a living.
- **Numbers over adjectives.** "30–70% below market," "SDS + COA on every lot," "verified in ~24h." Not "amazing deals."
- **Respect the regulation.** Reference SDS, COA, TDS, hazmat, EPA reuse naturally — it signals we know the industry.
- **No hype, no greenwash.** Sustainability is a real outcome of the transaction; state it plainly, don't preach.
- Sentences short. Active voice. British/US-neutral spelling acceptable; be consistent within a page.

## Design soul
- **Industrial + sustainable.** Deep teal (`--teal #0e6b5c`) for substance and trust; mint (`--mint #16c79a`) for the circular/fresh accent; amber (`--amber`) strictly for caution/hazmat and discounts.
- **Material, not abstract.** Imagery is drums, IBC totes, supersacks, molecular networks — the real objects of the trade — as clean inline SVG.
- **Documentation visible.** SDS/COA/TDS badges are a feature, not fine print.
- **Fast and legible.** Static, lightweight, high-contrast. Speed is a brand value (and an SEO one).

## Strategy soul: SEO-led, always
Growth comes from owning the *language of surplus chemicals* in search — every buyer and seller search term maps to a purpose-built, documented page. Content is data-driven and regenerable (`seo/keywords.json` → `build-seo.ps1`) so the surface area compounds and never goes stale. We win a fragmented, low-design niche on **structure, clarity, and trust**, not on ad spend.

## What we will not do
- List undocumented or misdescribed material.
- Use fear/greenwashing copy or fake scarcity timers.
- Touch a buyer's or seller's financial credentials — transactions stay between verified parties.
- Compromise compliance to close a deal faster.
