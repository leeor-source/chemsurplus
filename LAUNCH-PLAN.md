# ChemSurplus — Launch & Partnership Plan

*Who to onboard, who to sell to, and who does what to get this started. Grounded in market research (sources at bottom). Companion to [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) (strategy) and [SEO-GROWTH.md](SEO-GROWTH.md) (organic traffic).*

## The shape of the launch
**Model: supply-aggregated, demand-led.** We don't own inventory, so the real constraint is *demand quality*, not supply. Brokers are an easy "yes" (a free, qualified-demand channel with no software to build) — but they can't be shown value without a live catalog. So: **court supply first (lightly), then race to demand**, where the durable moat is owning the qualified-buyer relationship.

The research confirmed the wedge: **most incumbent brokers are credible and active but digitally primitive** (phone/email, no online catalog or RFQ). The few that *are* digital (Waste Optima, Knowde) prove buyers will use a clean catalog + RFQ + verified-spec trust layer. **Be the interface over messy broker supply — don't compete on inventory.**

Our edge is **industry-personalized aggregation across many brokers**, which no single broker-marketplace offers. **Camachem is a *prime* bulk supplier (not surplus) → deprioritize.**

## PART B — Demand: who buys surplus, and how to reach them

Launch in the **spec-tolerant lane**. Buyer profiles + real named companies + channels per industry:

### 🥇 Water treatment — *beachhead*
- **Buyers:** specialty formulators (scale/corrosion inhibitors, coagulants), toll/contract blenders, regional manufacturer-blender-distributors. Market **~$7.7B (2024)→$9.2B (2030)**, **660+ suppliers** = fragmented & price-driven.
- **Named:** Hawkins Inc., USALCO, CITCO Water, Hibrett Puratex, ChemCeed, CREMER ERZKONTOR NA, E&C Chemicals.
- **They buy:** alum, ferric chloride/sulfate, caustic, acids, hypochlorite, PEGs — on price, high spec tolerance.
- **Reach via:** **AWWA, WEF**, WQA · **WEFTEC** (20k+ attendees — largest), AWWA/WEF Utility Mgmt, WQTC · EPA Water-Treatment Supplier Locator, WaterWastewaterDirectory, Thomasnet.

### 🥇 Cleaning / detergents — *beachhead (thinnest margins)*
- **Buyers:** contract blenders / private-label manufacturers — the **most price-sensitive of all**; buy opportunistically.
- **Named:** ABC Compounding (400k sq ft, Atlanta), Gurtler Industries, Vitex Corp, Advanced Blending, Custom Chemical Formulators (CCFI), Alpha Chemical, Ashburn Chemical.
- **They buy:** commodity surfactants, builders, solvents, caustics, fragrances.
- **Reach via:** **ISSA** (11,000+ members), American Cleaning Institute · **ISSA Show N.A.** (Las Vegas, Nov 16–19 2026; 600+ exhibitors) · ISSA directory, PrivateLabelHub.

### ⚡ Plastics / polymers — *fastest conversion (already believes in surplus)*
- **Buyers:** resin compounders, molders, OEMs, reprocessors. **"Wide-spec"/off-spec resin is a normal, accepted purchase** — lowest education cost.
- **Named:** Mid Continent Plastics (wide-spec distributor), PolyUSA, Nexeo Plastics, PolyQuest.
- **They buy:** off-grade resins for packaging, containers, auto parts, electronics enclosures — on application-fit + price.
- **Reach via:** **PLASTICS (ex-SPI)**, APR · **NPE: The Plastics Show** · Thomasnet off-grade listings.

### 🎯 Paints & coatings — *high value, phase 2 (slightly spec-conservative)*
- **Buyers:** small/regional private-label paint makers, contract blenders, ink/adhesive formulators.
- **Named:** Nationwide Coatings, GDB International (buys surplus paint RM), RawMaterialsForPaint.com.
- **Why they care:** **TiO₂ = 20–30% of raw-material cost** → intense incentive to source surplus TiO₂, resins (alkyd/epoxy/polyester), pigments, solvents, driers.
- **Reach via:** **American Coatings Association** · **PCI Magazine, Coatings World** (+ distributor directory) · **American Coatings Show** (Indianapolis, May 5–7 2026).

### Cross-industry buyer channels
- **Thomasnet** — 2,200+ chemical suppliers / procurement pros; *no built-in RFQ* → our RFQ layer is the differentiator. Great for prospecting.
- **Alliance for Chemical Distribution (ACD, ex-NACD)** — 400+ distributor members who blend/repackage/formulate for 750k+ customers — both buy surplus *and* resell to our buyers.
- **LinkedIn** — titles: procurement/purchasing manager, R&D/formulation chemist, plant manager at SMB formulators & contract blenders.
- **Trust table-stakes** (match these): verified COA/SDS, "no mystery drums," clear off-spec labeling, 1–2 day response (Waste Optima / Knowde / ChemDirect standard).

**Beachhead call:** lead with **cleaning + water treatment** (price-sensitive demand wedge) and use **plastics wide-spec** as the fast-convert segment. Coatings is phase 2.

## PART C — Who does what: first 90 days

### Weeks 1–3 · Stand up minimum supply *(Founder / BD)*
1. Sign **3–5 anchor brokers** — prioritize **Allchem, Altiras, Sarren**; add **Tychem / Stobec** for breadth. Pitch: *"free qualified RFQs, you keep pricing and the customer, no software to build."*
2. Ingest their spec-tolerant inventory into **one clean catalog** with COA/SDS + verified-spec display ("no mystery drums").
3. Scope the pilot: **2 verticals (cleaning + water treatment)**, ~20–40 high-liquidity SKUs (solvents, caustics, acids, hypochlorite, coagulants, surfactants, PEGs; + a TiO₂/resin teaser for coatings).

### Weeks 3–8 · Demand-led pilot *(Founder / BD)*
4. Build a target list of **50–100 SMB buyers** (ABC, Gurtler, Vitex, CCFI, Advanced Blending; water blenders like Hibrett Puratex / ChemCeed-type regionals; wide-spec compounders like Mid Continent, PolyQuest). Source via Thomasnet, ACD member list, ISSA directory, LinkedIn.
5. Run a **concierge RFQ pilot**: buyer posts a need → we match against aggregated broker inventory → return 1–3 quotes with COA. Manually broker the first deals to learn liquidity & spec tolerance. Charge a transaction/referral fee; **don't gate the pilot on payments infra.**
6. Land the first **3–5 closed transactions** as proof points; capture testimonials + savings %.

### Weeks 8–13 · Prove the loop, then expand
7. Use pilot data to onboard **5–8 more brokers** (Providence for motivated near-expiry lots; SUR+/Sandrine/PPLC for breadth) — now there's demand to show *them*.
8. Plan **one demand event per vertical**: ISSA Show (cleaning), WEFTEC (water); American Coatings Show if coatings is greenlit.
9. Codify the **trust/quality SLA** (verified COA/SDS, off-spec labeling, response-time target) as the brand promise — the thing that differentiates us from the broker patchwork.

### KPIs (90 days)
Brokers signed (→5–8) · catalog SKUs live · qualified RFQs/week · RFQ→quote→close rate · avg buyer savings % · repeat buyers.

> Validate in BD calls: exact scale of smaller brokers (SUR+, Sandrine, PPLC); confirm Camachem is prime-only (deprioritize for surplus).
