Sell surplus chemicals — turn dead stock into cash

If you're holding excess, obsolete, off-spec or short-dated chemical inventory, you have three options: pay to dispose of it, let it tie up capital and warehouse space, or recover value by selling it. ChemSurplus is the fastest route to the third — we put your lot in front of qualified buyers across our verified broker network, with SDS/COA front and centre so serious buyers act quickly.

What you can sell

  • Overstock & over-orders — prime material you simply have too much of.
  • Off-spec & wide-spec — misses one prime parameter, performs in less demanding uses.
  • Short-dated / near-expiry — usable well within shelf life by higher-throughput buyers.
  • Discontinued SKUs & formulation-change orphans, and plant-closure / decommissioning inventory.

Seller FAQ

Selling surplus chemicals, answered

Who buys surplus chemicals?

Formulators, contract blenders, regional manufacturers and labs across coatings, cleaning, water treatment, plastics and more — sourcing quality chemistry 30–70% below market. We match your lot to the industries that use it.

What is my off-spec batch worth?

Typically 30–70% of prime value depending on the deviation, quantity, shelf life and packaging — almost always more than the cost of disposing of it. List it and we'll bring you buy-out or brokerage options.

Is it free to list?

Yes — listing is free. You keep pricing control and choose a fast buy-out or a brokerage deal that maximises recovery.

Do I have to handle hazmat shipping?

No — we match drums, totes, supersacks and bulk to compliant, hazmat-qualified carriers.
